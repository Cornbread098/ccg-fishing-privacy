# CCG Fishing – Account Deletion

CCG Fishing allows users to permanently delete their account and associated account profile data.

## Delete your account in the CCG Fishing app

1. Sign in to CCG Fishing.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Select **My Fishing Profile**.
4. Select **Delete Account**.
5. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.

Your CCG Fishing account and associated cloud profile data will be deleted.

## Request account deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to the CCG Fishing app, you may request deletion of your account by contacting CCG Fishing support.

Email: meyerp456@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your CCG Fishing account and include **CCG Fishing Account Deletion Request** in the subject line.

## Data deleted

When your account is deleted, the following account data is deleted:

- Firebase Authentication account
- Email address associated with the account
- CCG Fishing cloud fishing profile, including name, home fishing area, boat information, boat draft, target species, and fishing style

Data stored only locally on your device may remain on that device until the CCG Fishing app or its local app data is removed.

Some information may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal, or regulatory purposes.
